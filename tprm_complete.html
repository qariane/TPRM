How to narrate this in an interview
"I would score data sensitivity at 4 because they handle bank account numbers — that is regulated financial data. Business criticality at 3 because payroll runs biweekly — if they go down, we feel it within days. That puts inherent risk at 12, landing solidly in High tier, which means SIG Core, annual full assessment, and DPA required." That three-part narration — inputs, score, and downstream requirements — is the answer interviewers want.
